History & Heritage

BhashaVerse distinguishes documented historical events from oral community narratives and records still awaiting speaker verification.

Key documented script & photography milestones

  1. 1871: Nilgiri Toda photographic portraits documented by Madras School of Arts (Public domain plate in archive).
  2. 1925: Pandit Raghunath Murmu devises the Ol Chiki alphabet to represent Santali phonology accurately.
  3. 1936: Mangei Gomango creates the 24-character Sora Sompeng alphabet for Sora communities in eastern India.

Sample records, quotes and audio on this site are placeholders pending community verification.
