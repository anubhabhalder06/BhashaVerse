About BhashaVerse

Purpose

BhashaVerse documents India’s underrepresented and marginalised languages alongside manuscripts, oral histories, photographs, and regional cultural memory.

Methodology & honesty

Every entry indicates rights, source attribution, and verification status. Unverified items—such as scripts or audio still awaiting speaker guidance—remain clearly marked rather than filled with guessed figures.

Stewardship & partners

Community speakers, local families, educators, and cultural custodians guide naming conventions, orthography choices, restricted access rules, and takedown requests.

Acknowledgements & contact

All documentary plates displayed in this static prototype originate from cited Wikimedia Commons public-domain or open-licensed works. Questions or corrections may be sent via the Contact / Report page.

Sample records, quotes and audio on this site are placeholders pending community verification.
