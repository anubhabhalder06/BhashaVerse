About Matrika

Name & meaning

Matrika (मातृका) — pronounced MAA-tri-kaa — comes from Sanskrit mātṛkā, “little mother”. In the Sanskrit grammatical tradition, the letters of the alphabet are the “mothers” of all speech. It also reads as “mother tongue”.

Project goals & methodology

Matrika is an academic EPICS project building an accessible digital archive for India’s underrepresented and marginalised languages alongside manuscripts, oral histories, photographs, and regional knowledge.

Every record distinguishes stored, viewable, and reusable rights tiers. Unverified scripts, placeholder quotations, mock audio slots, and review statuses remain openly labelled rather than filled with guessed figures.
