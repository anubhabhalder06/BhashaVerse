Copyright & Reuse Policy

Stored, viewable & reusable are distinct

Digitisation on Matrika does not imply unrestricted public permission. Every record badge documents its exact rights tier:

  • Public domain: Out-of-copyright works permitted for viewing and download with citation.
  • Openly licensed: Shared under Creative Commons open licences (such as CC BY 2.5, CC BY-SA 3.0/4.0) requiring exact creator credit.
  • Permission granted: Shown with community or creator consent; downloadable only when access terms specify open.
  • Restricted: View-only metadata inside Matrika; downloads remain disabled pending elder/custodian approval.
  • Metadata only: Catalogue citation shown with no embedded file or preview.
