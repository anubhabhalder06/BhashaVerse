Contribution Guidelines

Consent & dignity

Submit photographs, voices, and stories only when every identifiable speaker or subject—and parents/guardians for minors—has granted informed consent for archival storage and display.

Preferred naming & spelling

Use self-identified community language names and respectful descriptions. Indicate uncertain classifications or scripts openly so reviewers can verify directly with speakers.

Attribution & rights

State creator credit, source provenance, and chosen licence (or request restricted access / anonymity prior to review).
