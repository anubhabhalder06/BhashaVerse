Contribution Guidelines

Consent & cultural safety

Submit recordings, photographs, and stories only when every identifiable speaker or participant—and parents or guardians for minors—has granted informed consent for archival review and display. Never submit sensitive village coordinates, sacred rituals, or restricted knowledge without elder authorisation.

Community-preferred terminology

Use self-identified community language names and respectful phrasing. Do not use demeaning historical labels; describe underrepresented or marginalised languages plainly and mark unverified scripts or glosses openly.

Attribution, moderation & withdrawal

All submissions enter moderation as pending and are reviewed for accuracy, rights, consent, and safety before any public display. Contributors retain the right to request clarification, restricted access, corrections, or withdrawal at any time.
