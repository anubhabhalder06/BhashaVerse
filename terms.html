Terms of Use

Respectful research & educational use

Matrika provides open metadata and openly licensed materials for non-harmful linguistic, cultural, and educational study. Viewing an archival item does not grant permission to redistribute restricted or permission-only materials.

Takedown, correction & consent withdrawal

Any community speaker, custodian, or rights holder may request immediate review, attribution updates, access restriction, or removal of any record through the Contact / Report page.

Prototype notice

Matrika Review 1 operates entirely in the browser with no external data storage outside your device.
