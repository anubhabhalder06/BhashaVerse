Contribute to BhashaVerse

All submissions enter moderation as pending and are reviewed for accuracy, rights, consent, and cultural sensitivity before publication. Read guidelines.

1. Submit with rights & consent → 2. Reviewed with the community → 3. Published with attribution

Submitted in local browser preview. In production, submissions are queued for reviewer approval. Inspect queue mockup →

How moderation protects communities

  1. Every item is stored initially as pending, never published live immediately.
  2. Reviewers check accuracy, spelling, rights clearance, explicit consent, and privacy (especially photographs involving minors).
  3. Reviewers may approve, request clarification from the contributor, restrict access, or decline the entry.
  4. Approved items display source citation, rights label, and contributor attribution.

Sample records, quotes and audio on this site are placeholders pending community verification.
