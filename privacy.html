Privacy

Prototype browser-only storage

This Review 1 prototype of Matrika has no server database, no analytics, and no tracking scripts. Nothing you type—including contribution previews, bookmarks, theme preferences, wordmark pause state, or Matrika Assistant messages—is sent outside your own browser.

Local browser keys used

  • matrika:theme — light paper or dark ink preference.
  • matrika:wordmark-paused — saved wordmark animation pause state.
  • matrika:bookmarks — record IDs saved via Bookmark buttons.
  • matrika:contributions — demonstration form metadata stored locally (file contents are never read or saved).

Intended Review 2 collection & withdrawal

When a moderated backend launches in future reviews, contributor contact details will be collected solely for verification and consent follow-up, never published without consent, and removable upon takedown or withdrawal request via Contact / Report.
