Privacy

Browser-local storage

This static edition of Matrika uses no server database, analytics, or tracking scripts. Bookmarks, theme selection, and contribution queue entries remain inside your browser.

Local browser keys used

  • matrika:theme — surface palette setting.
  • matrika:bookmarks — record IDs saved via Bookmark buttons.
  • matrika:contributions — contribution form metadata stored locally (file contents are never read or saved).

Custodial submission privacy & withdrawal

Where contributor contact details are recorded for moderation review and consent follow-up, they remain private and may be withdrawn at any time via Contact / Report.
